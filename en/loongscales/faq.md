# FAQ

**Q: Does LoongScales need to be installed?**
A: No. It runs directly in the browser; it can also be embedded into host products like DragonBones.

**Q: Will my work be lost?**
A: No. The document is auto-saved in the browser's local database and survives refresh/close. For important works, also keep a PSD backup.

**Q: Which layers can sync to DragonBones?**
A: Only **raster layers** sync; text, vector, adjustment, fill, group do not.

**Q: Why can't some layers be deleted/merged?**
A: Those are layers already bound to DragonBones (blue link icon 🔗), protected from manual delete/merge to avoid breaking animation references.

**Q: Can AI-generated images be undone?**
A: Yes. All AI operations go into the undo stack; press `Ctrl+Z` to roll back. The LoongCoins record is kept but the canvas reverts.

**Q: Insufficient balance / generation failed?**
A: Top up when the balance is insufficient; on failure read the reason on the button (policy violation, timeout, network, etc.), click once to return to normal and retry.

**Q: Do background removal / split cost LoongCoins?**
A: Yes, and they are recorded in the LoongCoins details, viewable anytime.

**Q: Can I continue my file in Photoshop?**
A: Yes. Export PSD to open in PS; you can also import PS's PSD/PSB into LoongScales to keep editing.

**Q: How do I split a combined image into multiple layers?**
A: Select the combined layer → the "Split Image" tab in the AI panel, or "One-click Split" in the Layers panel (for DragonBones sprite sheets).
