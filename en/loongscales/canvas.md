# Canvas & View

## Zoom & pan

- **Zoom**: scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in/out centered on the cursor. Range from **5% to 2000%** — enough for both detail and the big picture.
- **Pan**: hold **Space** and drag, or drag with the right mouse button, to temporarily switch to the hand tool and move the canvas.
- **Zoom percentage**: the top-left corner, where the horizontal and vertical rulers meet, shows the current zoom percentage.
- **Fit view**: click the top-left corner where the horizontal and vertical rulers meet (which shows the current zoom percentage) to scale the canvas content to exactly fill the window.

## Canvas size & presets

In the **Canvas** area of the right Layers panel you can set canvas width/height (range **64–4096 px**). Several preset groups are also provided — pick instead of typing:

- **Web**: avatar, leaderboard, half-page, rectangle, skyscraper and other ad sizes
- **Display**: XGA to DCI 4K
- **Phone**: mainstream ratios like 18.5:9
- **Misc**: CD cover, etc.
- **Fit to layer**: with a layer selected, a fit button appears to the right of the canvas width/height fields; click it to auto-fit the canvas to the selected layer's width and height.

## Rulers & guides

- **Rulers** sit above and to the left of the canvas, showing ticks and the current pointer position (a blue triangle marker).
- You can drag out **guides** to help alignment.

## Grid & snapping

- Moving a layer auto-"clicks" to the guides, canvas edges, center, or other layers' edges and centers.
- Snapping can also align "edges, gaps, equal spacing" — handy for neat layouts.
- In the transform tool (R) properties bar you can set the snap grid size (0 disables it).
